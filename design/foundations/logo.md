**The files.** The horizontal lockup (`OpenMined-Logo*.svg`), the stacked lockup (`OpenMined-Logo-Stacked*.svg`) and the icon mark (`OpenMined-Icon.svg`), served at `/logos/`. Each lockup comes as:

- **Full color**, for light grounds.
- **Reversed** (`-Dark`), full color with light lettering, for dark grounds.
- **Ghost**, a translucent version for dark grounds where the logo should recede.
- **Mono white** and **mono black**, for one-color uses: print, stamps, a photo where the colors would clash.

PNG renders (1200px wide) of the reversed and mono black lockups and the icon are in `/logos/raster/`, for places that can't take SVG, such as Google Docs.

**Use.** On a page that changes mode, show full color in light and reversed in dark. Use the logo files as they are (*The logo keeps its own colors*); never draw, generate or recolor the mark. The icon mark stands alone where the name is already on the page, such as a favicon or a footer.

**Not yet defined.** Clearspace and minimum sizes, as in v1.
