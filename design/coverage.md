**How to read it.** *Covered*: use it as it is. *Partly*: the values and some pieces work; expect to make calls the system hasn't made yet, and tell us which. *Not yet*: nothing specific; use the foundations and tell us.

**Tell us.** If something you need isn't covered, or the system got in your way, that's the most useful feedback there is: it decides what comes next (*Overview*, "Feedback").
