**Rule.** Violet and blue never sit next to each other in a chart, and never mark the two sides of a comparison (before/after, A/B).

**Why.** Under the most common color blindness (deuteranopia) the pair is nearly indistinguishable. The chart series order already keeps them apart.

**A working rule**: it may evolve with the palette.
