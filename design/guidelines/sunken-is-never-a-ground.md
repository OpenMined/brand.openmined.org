---
type: Guideline
title: Sunken is never a ground
description: The sunken level is a recess inside a surface, sized to a control. Never a section, band or panel of content.
status: working
tags: [surfaces]
tokens: [--surface-sunken]
---

**Rule.** Use `--surface-sunken` only for a recess inside a surface: a field, a toggle or progress track, a badge, a hover wash. Never for a page region, a band or a panel holding content.

**Why.** A recess needs a surface around it. Used as a large background, sunken breaks the elevation model and darkens the page.

**Do.** Put a sunken track inside a card.

**Don't.** Make a whole section, sidebar or content panel sunken.
