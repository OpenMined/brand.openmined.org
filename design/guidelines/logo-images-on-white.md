title: Logo images sit on white
description: A logo that comes as an image file, such as the press room's, sits on a white plate in either mode. Vector partner marks follow the mode instead.
status: released
tags: [graphics]
---

**Rule.** An organization's logo supplied as an image (a PNG or JPEG, such as the logos in the press room on openmined.org's home page) sits on white (`--white`) in either page mode: a press card or logo tile is a white plate.

**Why.** An image can't change with the mode. Logos are drawn for white, and on gray-50 or a dark surface their colors shift and some lose their edges.

**Not partner marks.** The vector marks in `public/logos/partners/` follow the color mode instead: see [Partner marks follow the color mode](#guidelines-partner-marks-follow-the-mode). Where a press logo has a vector partner mark, prefer the mark.
