type: Guideline
title: Avoid trends, AI design trends especially
description: A treatment that reads as "of this moment" is a cost. One that reads as AI-generated house style is the worst case.
status: working
tags: [overview, surfaces]
---

**Rule.** Before adopting a new treatment, check whether it reads as a current trend, and especially as the look of AI-generated design. If it does, don't adopt it.

**Why.** The brand is made by people using AI, and it can't afford to look like its tooling. A trend dates the brand; an AI house style makes it look generated.

**Example.** Borders on everything is the first worked case, and the reason the system is dividers-only. Expect others.

**Check.** Apply this to every new element, graphic and layout, before it becomes part of the system.
