**Context.** The website's type system is ahead of the brand's: fluid, tokenized, with readable-content rhythm. The brand has fixed pixel sizes and no type tokens.

**Decision.** The brand takes the foundation (fluid scale tokens, the clamp mechanism, family aliases, leading, tracking, heading rhythm, measure) and drops the complex overrides, which stay in the website.

**Why.** The website's system is refined but tied to its content model. The brand should be a solid starting point, not a transplant, the same call as the grid: the brand offers a default, projects diverge.
