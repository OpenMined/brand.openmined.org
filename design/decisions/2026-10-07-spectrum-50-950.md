**Context.** Gray had 17 inherited steps (00–1000) and each hue 7 (100–700). Both ran light to dark, but gray 300 matched hue 200 in lightness, and 17 felt arbitrary.

**Decision.** Every scale runs 50, 100–900, 950 (eleven steps, light to dark). White and black are separate tokens. Three layers: primitives → tokens (`gray-N`, `blue-N`) → roles. Further x50 steps are added only when a round needs one, with a job attached; existing names never move.

**Alternatives.** Keep 17 steps (with or without the two unused ones), renumber evenly, count from 1, name steps by lightness (rejected: higher would mean lighter, reversing the hues' direction), a 20-step gray (held as a second option).

**Consequences.** Agreed values were pinned at their new step; the hue key moved from 400 to 500. New steps are provisional. Three free middle grays leave room for text and lines.
