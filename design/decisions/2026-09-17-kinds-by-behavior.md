**Context.** Everything at the sunken level was one kind, "inset". Then three things at that level needed three different shadow behaviors.

**Decision.** Split by behavior: **control** (a recess you operate; casts at rest), **recess** (a recess you don't touch; never casts), **field** (flat until focus; the recess is the focus state).

**Why.** A taxonomy keyed to a position keeps producing exceptions. *A kind must name a question, not a position.*
