**Context.** *Examples show the rules in use* (2026-10-29) said to copy examples freely and to report any difference from the guidance as feedback. Examples are drawn from work in progress (versions of openmined.org pages, SyftHub), and some predate rules released since: in the skill's evaluation on 2026-11-06, two runs started a hero from the SyftHub example and found it spaced with margins where stacks use a gap, and two eyebrow classes of its own where eyebrows take the eyebrow style. Both runs followed the guidance, as the 2026-10-29 record says, but "copy freely" invites carrying such drift into new work, and a full feedback issue for each known lag would crowd the inbox.

**Decision.** Design lead, 2026-11-06.
- **Examples are visual reference; the guidance is the ruleset.** Examples may lag the guidance while they are brought up to date.
- **Start from an example, don't copy it whole:** take what it shows, checking each part against the guidance files it names.
- **A difference is said in one line**, in the report or the reply: what the example does, what the guidance says, what was done. Feedback is filed only when the guidance has no rule to follow (a gap), not for an example that lags.
- The rest of 2026-10-29 stands: examples have no status, each names what it shows, they use only released parts, website examples share the example kit.

**Alternatives.** Bring every example up to the current guidance before launch: it is the goal, but not in the time before launch, and examples will keep coming from work in progress. Keep "copy freely": it spreads drift. A full issue for every lagging example: noise for a lag that is already known.

**Revisit when.** Every example matches the current guidance: then a new record replaces this one and examples return to showing the rules as they stand.
